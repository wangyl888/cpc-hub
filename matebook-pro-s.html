Section 03

Key Marketing Highlights & Core Innovations

7 industry-first innovations claimed by Huawei

01

World's Lightest 14" Full-Metal Laptop

Industry 1st

Only 798g, 11.9mm thin. Achieved through four concurrent innovations: magnesium-lithium alloy chassis, mortise-and-tenon unibody frame, compact 3-section motherboard, and 10% high-silicon battery.

02

Lingdun Privacy Display: One-Zone Dual-Pixel

Industry 1st

World's first flexible OLED privacy display. Proprietary one-zone dual-pixel architecture delivers identical resolution, brightness, and color in both Share and Privacy modes — ending the "blur when private" trade-off.

03

Wi-Fi 7+ Quad-Antenna Design

Industry 1st

World's first ultra-slim laptop with Wi-Fi 7+ and a 4-antenna layout for true 360° omnidirectional signal. Stable connection up to 1,000 meters in open environments (lab conditions).

04

Kirin XE90 High-Efficiency Processor

Huawei in-house PC chip. +23% single-core, +25% efficiency, +40% NPU vs Kirin X90. Ark Engine + Tianquan Window Engine: PPT open +30%, file extraction +25%, spreadsheet +20%.

05

Celia AI Tasks: On-Device Agent

New

Local AI Agent: task decomposition → planning → tool invocation → delivery. Offline meeting transcription with AI speaker separation, deep research, zero-code webpage generation.

06

Extended Battery + Reverse Fast Charging

First ultra-slim with 10% high-silicon battery (+15% energy density). Three-layer power optimization adds ~5.7 extra hours for a total of 18h. 66W reverse charging for phone/tablet.

07

HarmonyOS Cross-Device Ecosystem & Privacy

Tap-to-transfer, phone mirroring, tablet as extended display. Hardware privacy: physical switch, AI deepfake detection, encrypted file sharing with auto-expiry. CC EAL5+ full-chain certified.

Consensus Strengths (6 Points)

●
Portability
798g delivers a genuinely transformative carry experience — ~1/3 lighter than mainstream 14" laptops. Unanimously called an irreplaceable advantage for frequent travelers.
●
Display Quality
264 PPI + 120Hz + P3 + ΔE<1 places it at the top tier of 14" ultrabooks; praised for both productivity and multimedia.
●
Lingdun Privacy Screen
Privacy mode shows no perceptible loss in brightness or sharpness — an industry breakthrough. Physical toggle rated more reliable than software alternatives.
●
Battery Life
Real-world office use: 10–14 hours, top tier for ultrabooks. High-silicon battery + power management exceed class expectations.
●
OS Smoothness
HarmonyOS daily fluency meets or exceeds Windows. Multi-task switching lag-free; large file operations noticeably faster.
●
Ecosystem Synergy
HarmonyOS cross-device collaboration identified as the core differentiator; clear productivity gains for Huawei ecosystem users.
✗

Key Weaknesses (5 Points)

●
Port Selection
Only 2 × USB-C; no USB-A, HDMI, or SD card slot. A dock is effectively mandatory for multi-peripheral setups — cited as the top hardware shortcoming.
●
Software Ecosystem
HarmonyOS app library still has gaps: certain finance/accounting systems, legacy CAD, and industry-specific tools lack native HarmonyOS versions.
●
Performance Ceiling
20W max TDP insufficient for sustained video editing or 3D rendering — noticeably behind the sibling MateBook Pro (40W).
●
Price Premium
Privacy Screen from ¥10,299; Collector's at ¥14,999. Pure performance does not match MacBook Air M5 at this price; premium justified only by differentiating features.
●
AI Feature OTA Gap
Several advertised AI features (Celia Tasks, new charging UI) unavailable at launch; require post-launch OTA updates. Some reviewers flagged this as misleading marketing.
