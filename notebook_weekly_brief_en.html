Industry Intelligence

Cost — Memory + Chip Double Squeeze Compressing Margins

🔴 High Priority
The memory shortage is the single highest-priority cost risk. SK Hynix's CEO has warned that 2027 will be the worst supply year in industry history; HBM is cannibalizing conventional DRAM capacity, pushing memory's share of entry-level laptop BOM to 80% (Intel CEO, AI Infrastructure Summit). AMD has notified partners of a ~10% price increase on AI accelerators, consumer Radeon GPUs, and motherboard chipsets effective Q4 2026, tracing back to TSMC raising wafer prices 5–10% on sub-5nm nodes. A further TSMC hike of up to 10% is planned for 2027. Ryzen CPUs are not yet on the list, but analysts flag the risk of follow-on increases. Smaller OEMs are resorting to extremes: Framework is placing "blind orders" without confirmed pricing or delivery dates; Jolla has designed dual-motherboard configurations to hedge against specific package shortages.

Supply — Capacity Realignment; Lead Times Stretched to Six Months

🟠 Important
Multiple Taiwanese and US-based notebook manufacturers are shifting production capacity back from Southeast Asia to mainland China — a directional reversal of the "China+1" strategy pursued over the past several years. Simultaneously, shortages of CPUs, PCBs, and power ICs have stretched lead times at leading industrial PC vendors to six months, with large order backlogs rolling into 2027. For smaller OEMs, securing a memory allocation has become existential — more important than negotiating price. Counterfeit and refurbished chip infiltration is rising.

Policy — China's 15th Five-Year Plan Takes Effect; Global Semiconductor Onshoring Accelerates

🟡 Monitor
On September 15, China's MIIT and NDRC jointly issued the Electronics Manufacturing Industry 15th Five-Year Development Plan, designating AI PCs as a priority category, targeting industry revenue above ¥30 trillion by 2030 and R&D intensity of 3.5%. The NDRC requires domestic sourcing rates above 80% by 2027; the Ministry of Education requires all primary and secondary schools to be equipped with notebooks by end of 2026. A new laptop energy efficiency standard (covering entry-level discrete GPU models) has taken effect. China Consumer Association data shows laptop complaints rose 35% YoY in 2025, a five-year high — regulatory scrutiny is tightening.
