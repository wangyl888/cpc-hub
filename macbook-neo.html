Section 03

Key Marketing Highlights and Core Innovations

What Apple says makes the MacBook Neo special

01

Apple's Most Affordable Mac Laptop Ever

Historic

At $699 ($499 for education), the MacBook Neo breaks Apple's pricing floor for laptops, opening macOS to a completely new audience for the first time in Apple's history.

02

A18 Pro Chip — No Performance Compromise

Despite the entry price, the MacBook Neo uses the same A18 Pro chip as iPhone 16 Pro. 6-core CPU, 5-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine — genuinely fast for all everyday tasks and light creative work.

03

Apple Intelligence — On-Device AI

New

Full Apple Intelligence support: Writing Tools, Image Playground, Genmoji, Priority Notifications, and Siri with personal context — all running on-device with Private Cloud Compute for sensitive tasks.

04

All-Day Battery Life

Up to 16 hours of video streaming on a 36.5Wh battery — thanks to the efficiency of the A18 Pro chip. Real-world testing confirms 10 to 13 hours in daily mixed use.

05

iPhone Mirroring and Cross-Device Continuity

iPhone Mirroring lets you use your iPhone directly on the Mac screen. Handoff, AirDrop, Universal Clipboard, Sidecar, and AirPlay to Mac all work seamlessly out of the box with no configuration.

06

Premium Aluminum Build at Entry Price

Full CNC-machined aluminum unibody — no plastic, no flex, no creaks. Available in four colors: Midnight, Starlight, Sky Blue, and Pink. A premium feel at an unprecedented price point.

07

Privacy and Security by Design

Private Cloud Compute ensures AI tasks stay private. Touch ID on the $799 model. Secure Enclave, FileVault, and Gatekeeper all built-in. Hardware microphone disconnect switch on all models.

X

Key Weaknesses (5 Points)

-
No OLED, No ProMotion
60Hz LCD IPS display is a clear step down from MacBook Air. No 120Hz, no OLED, no True Tone at base model. Noticeable when compared side by side with competitors.
-
Only 8GB RAM — No Upgrade Path
8GB unified memory is the sole option with no upgrade path. Multi-tasking with many browser tabs, large spreadsheets, or any video editing can feel constrained.
-
Minimal Port Selection
Only 2 USB-C ports (one USB 3, one USB 2 speed only) — no MagSafe, no HDMI, no SD card. A hub is necessary for most real-world workflows.
-
No Touch ID on Base Model
The $699 base model ships without Touch ID. Only the $799 model includes the Magic Keyboard with Touch ID — a notable omission at any price.
-
sRGB Only — No P3 Wide Color
No P3 wide color gamut. The display covers sRGB only, making it unsuitable for professional photo or video color grading work.
