Section 03

Key Marketing Highlights & Core Innovations

7 industry-first innovations claimed by Huawei

01

World's Lightest 14" Full-Metal Laptop

Industry 1st

Only 798g, 11.9mm thin. Achieved through four concurrent innovations: magnesium-lithium alloy chassis, mortise-and-tenon unibody frame, compact 3-section motherboard, and 10% high-silicon battery.

02

Lingdun Privacy Display: One-Zone Dual-Pixel

Industry 1st

World's first flexible OLED privacy display. Proprietary one-zone dual-pixel architecture delivers identical resolution, brightness, and color in both Share and Privacy modes — ending the "blur when private" trade-off.

03

Wi-Fi 7+ Quad-Antenna Design

Industry 1st

World's first ultra-slim laptop with Wi-Fi 7+ and a 4-antenna layout for true 360° omnidirectional signal. Stable connection up to 1,000 meters in open environments (lab conditions).

04

Kirin XE90 High-Efficiency Processor

Huawei in-house PC chip. +23% single-core, +25% efficiency, +40% NPU vs Kirin X90. Ark Engine + Tianquan Window Engine: PPT open +30%, file extraction +25%, spreadsheet +20%.

05

Celia AI Tasks: On-Device Agent

New

Local AI Agent: task decomposition → planning → tool invocation → delivery. Offline meeting transcription with AI speaker separation, deep research, zero-code webpage generation.

06

Extended Battery + Reverse Fast Charging

First ultra-slim with 10% high-silicon battery (+15% energy density). Three-layer power optimization adds ~5.7 extra hours for a total of 18h. 66W reverse charging for phone/tablet.

07

HarmonyOS Cross-Device Ecosystem & Privacy

Tap-to-transfer, phone mirroring, tablet as extended display. Hardware privacy: physical switch, AI deepfake detection, encrypted file sharing with auto-expiry. CC EAL5+ full-chain certified.

Summary: The MateBook Pro S is the most well-rounded 14" ultralight laptop on the market. Its 798g weight, top-tier OLED display, and excellent battery life make it the go-to choice for mobile professionals. Key weaknesses are the 20W performance ceiling and HarmonyOS ecosystem — not suitable for heavy creative work or users dependent on specific Windows software.
